# Mentorship Thread — Growth

*Living doc; worked across sessions. Charter: [[BRAINIA-MENTOR-CHARTER]].*

## The goal
What you want to get better at next — a skill, a discipline, a depth — and the path to it. Learn by doing, using your real work and projects as practice.

## Starting level
> *Be honest about where you are: what you already do well, and what's still shallow.*

## Where the edges likely are
> *The specific gaps between where you are and where you want to be.*

## Practice surfaces you already own
> *Real projects or contexts where you can practice this for free, instead of in the abstract.*

## Open questions
- Are you learning this for application, or for credibility/positioning? The answer changes the plan.
- Depth-first on one thing, or breadth across several?
